🎉 I am delighted to share that [*Controllable Egocentric Video Generation via Occlusion-Aware Sparse 3D Hand Joints*](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.11755) has been accepted to [ECCV 2026](https://eccv.ecva.net/). Led by Chenyangguang Zhang and Botao Ye, together with Marc Pollefeys and Xi Wang, we steer egocentric video generation with sparse 3D hand joints.
