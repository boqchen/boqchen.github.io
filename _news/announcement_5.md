My co-first-authored paper [Controllable Egocentric Video Generation via Occlusion-Aware Sparse 3D Hand Joints](https://arxiv.org/abs/2603.11755) is accepted by ECCV 2026. It generates egocentric videos controlled by sparse 3D hand joints and transfers to robotic hands.
